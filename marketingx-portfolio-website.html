About Shahnawaz Ahmed

Founder of MarketingX — Digital Solutions & Business Growth. Helping businesses build stronger digital presences with websites, eCommerce, apps, SEO and advertising.

I'm Shahnawaz Ahmed — I plan it, I build it, I support it

I founded MarketingX on a simple belief: good digital work should be both beautifully designed and commercially effective. When you work with MarketingX, you work directly with me — no hand-offs, no account managers — so the person who plans your project is the person who builds it, and the person you can call after it launches.

My work spans Shopify and WordPress development, custom and AI-assisted websites, web and mobile apps, technical and local SEO, and paid advertising across Meta, Google and TikTok. That breadth matters: your website, your search visibility and your ads should all pull in the same direction, and I build them that way.

I take on projects I can personally stand behind. If something won't help your business, I'll tell you — and if I recommend it, it's because it serves a goal we agreed together.

My working approach

Every project starts with discovery — your goals, your customers, your competitors. From there I agree a written scope, timeline and quotation before any build work begins, keep you updated at each milestone, and test everything across devices before launch. You approve each stage, so there are no surprises at the end.

My principles

  • Quality over shortcuts — if it isn't tested, it isn't finished
  • Plain-English communication — no jargon, no silence
  • Honest advice — including when the answer is "you don't need that"
  • Support after launch — your project keeps improving, not ageing
